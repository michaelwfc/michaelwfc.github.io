# Karpathy AI LLM Wiki

## Key Takeaways

- The source describes a pattern for building a persistent personal or team wiki with LLMs.
- The workflow centers on ingesting sources, writing synthesized pages into the wiki, and maintaining cross-references over time.
- The model is expected to update the index and log as part of the ingestion process.
